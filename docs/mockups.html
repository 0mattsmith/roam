Android Auto

Rendered by Android Auto from Roam's browse tree and media session — Roam supplies the icon, tabs, artwork, item styles and custom actions. Layout, type and button geometry are the platform's.
What Roam controls here: the app badge, the four root tabs and their icons, list-vs-grid per node, all artwork, the accent colour, and the two custom action buttons (♥ love, shuffle). What it doesn't: everything else — every media app gets this same template, which is exactly why it feels familiar.
